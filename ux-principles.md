## Purpose

Stablish the principles that need to be followed for creating a good UI for the users.
This is a high level list, so it doesn't include things like "A design system" or "Reusable components". Those are technical requirement.
This list was created by Chatgpt. I checked and filtered by myself to keep only those that I consider  useful. 

## Core Principles

1. Make the work fast.
2. Make the system's state obvious.
3. Keep the interface consistent.
4. Make data easy to find and manipulate.
5. Design around real user behavior and tasks.

## UX/UI Criteria for an Administrative / ERP Web Application

1. Efficiency of use
   Minimize unnecessary clicks, typing, and steps, especially for repetitive tasks.

2. Clarity and orientation
   Users should always know where they are, what they are viewing, and what they can do.

3. Consistency
Use consistent components, terminology, icons, behaviors, and visual conventions throughout the application.

4. Simple and well-structured forms
Remove unnecessary fields, group related information, and organize complex processes logically.

5. Immediate feedback
Clearly communicate loading states, successful operations, failures, and system actions.

6. Visual hierarchy
Clearly distinguish important information and actions from secondary elements without unnecessary visual clutter.

7. Flexible workflows
Allow users to complete tasks efficiently and move backward or forward without losing their work or context.

8. Progressive disclosure
Show essential information first and reveal secondary details when needed.

9. Role-based interfaces
Adapt navigation, functionality, and available actions according to the user's role and responsibilities.

10. Accessibility
Support keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, visible focus states, clear labels, and non-color-dependent information.

11. User- and workflow-centered design
    Design around how users actually perform their work rather than around the application's database structure.
